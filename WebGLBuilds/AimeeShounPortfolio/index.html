03 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE FILES

AIMEE SHOUN

I SPEAK
ART &
CODE.

I'm an AR/VR and Interactive 3D Developer with a passion for creating immersive, engaging, and visually compelling digital experiences. With a Bachelor's degree in Animation & Game Development from Utah Valley University, where I graduated Cum Laude, I combine creative design with technical development to bring interactive ideas to life.

As an Augmented Reality Specialist and Game Developer at Stride K12, I've designed and developed interactive educational games, 3D environments, and augmented reality experiences across science, mathematics, English Language Arts, and social studies. My work spans the development process, from concept and 3D asset integration to programming, gameplay mechanics, user interfaces, optimization, and deployment.

I specialize in Unity, Unreal Engine, Three.js, WebGL, JavaScript, HTML/CSS, and C#, with additional experience in Blender, Autodesk Maya, and AI-assisted development workflows. I'm comfortable working independently, solving technical challenges, and transforming creative concepts into fully functional interactive experiences.

My projects include Fun Sweets Factory, Mystic Manor, Strider Voyager Science, Grammar Glider, and Immersive History Experiences. I also contributed development to ELA Village, a game-based learning ecosystem that received a Silver Medal at the 2025 International Serious Play Awards.

While my professional background is rooted in educational technology, my skills extend into game development, creative technology, immersive experiences, and interactive web applications. I enjoy exploring new technologies, building engaging digital worlds, and creating experiences that connect creativity with functionality.

I'm interested in opportunities involving Unity Development, AR/VR, Three.js, WebGL, Interactive 3D Development, Game Development, and Creative Technology.
