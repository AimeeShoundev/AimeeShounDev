WELCOME TO REWIND

A shelf in your space.

  1. Place the shelf

    Open on a compatible Android phone in Chrome. Choose “Place in my room,” allow AR access, and slowly scan an open, well-lit floor. Place the shelf when the gold ring appears.

  2. Pick a tape

    Tap a cover or choose one from Browse tapes. It moves in front of you while your room stays visible.

  3. Take a closer look

    Drag in any direction to rotate it. Use Front, Back, or Auto-spin. Pinch or use + and − to change its distance.

  4. Put it back

    Choose “Return to shelf,” then pick another tape. Use Move shelf to scan and place it somewhere new.

About room AR

Full room AR requires WebXR hit testing and touch overlays, available in Chrome on supported Android devices with Google Play Services for AR. iPhone/iPad Safari does not support this interactive room AR mode. You can explore the same tapes here in 3D preview.

Keyboard: use Browse tapes to choose a cover; arrow keys rotate a held tape; +/− change distance; Escape returns it. Camera permission is requested only when you start AR.
