How to play

The host clicks Start Race. Drive your car through all checkpoints in order, complete three laps, and cross the finish line first! Players can join during a race as spectators until the next round.

Computer: W / Up to accelerate, S / Down to reverse, A/D to steer, Space to brake.

Phone: On-screen accelerator, steering, reverse and BRAKE controls.

Share your room code to invite friends. Race positions, lap progress, and chat are synced for everyone in the room.
