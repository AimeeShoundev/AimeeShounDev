How to play

The host clicks Start Race. Follow the glowing checkpoint markers in order, complete three laps, and cross the finish line first! Players can join during a race as spectators until the next round.

Computer: WASD or arrow keys to move, Space to jump, drag to rotate the camera, mouse wheel to zoom.

Phone: On-screen movement buttons and JUMP. Drag the scene to rotate the camera.

Share your room code to invite friends. Race positions, lap progress, and chat are synced for everyone in the room.
